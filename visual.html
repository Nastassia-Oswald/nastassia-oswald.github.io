Tapedeck

My podcast player, and its UI kit · 2026 · v3

The app

A podcast player built around series: long history runs, multi-part stories and private Patreon feeds. I designed it for how I actually listen, with sleep and meditation shows kept apart from daily listening, and I keep changing it as I use it.

Playlists, Topics and Settings sit on the left of the tab bar. Today is a round brushed-metal button on the right.

Sorrel

A field guide for foragers

The app

Sorrel helps people find, identify and use wild plants: identify a plant, cook or prepare it, see where it grows, and keep a journal of what you find. It has 4 main screens: Identify, Recipes, Map and Journal.

It started as a portfolio piece about component libraries generated with AI from a moodboard and a short brief. Now I’m building it as a real app.

Dogear

Audiobooks and ebooks, on phones, tablets and e-ink readers

The app

Dogear is a listening and reading app for audiobooks and ebooks. Your library is sorted by genre, search covers titles, authors, chapters and words, and the You tab tracks your year: time with books, a reading goal and a sticker book you fill as you go.

The idea for the look: quiet pages, loud little stickers. Paper-white surfaces and soft gradients, with small full-colour stickers that each carry one meaning. It has 3 themes: light, dark and e-ink.

App concept · design system

Canopy

Plan smarter. Spend less.

The app

Canopy is a road trip planner for long drives, built around saving money. It is not a navigation app: you plan before you leave. Enter a route and Canopy finds the cheapest fuel, rest stops, food and places to stay along the way, and shows how much you save compared with stopping anywhere.

Rest stops show what matters on the road: whether the toilets are free or paid, if they are clean, and alerts from other drivers, like closed toilets or a station out of diesel.

Sample screens

Component library

A calm, neutral palette where the only loud colours are status: green for positive, pink for alerts. 42dot Sans in 4 weights, filter pills, a bottom nav, stop cards for fuel, rest, food and stays, and a savings badge on anything below the area average.
